There are 2 games in this program:
    - Scoundrel
    - Donsol (with 3 difficulties)

Scoundrel rules:
    - Jokerless deck without red J,Q,K, and A cards.
    - Starting and max HP is 20.
    - A room is 4 cards.
    - Clubs are weapons, Hearts are potions, everything else is a monster.
    - J is 11, Q is 12, K is 13 and A is 14
    - You cannot attack monsters of the same or higher level than the last monster you have killed with your weapon.
    - Doing so or attacking bare handed will result in max damage taken
    - Damage taken otherwise is DMG = Enemy - Weapon
    - You clear a room by clearing 3 cards, one remaining that carries over.
    - A room is skippable only if you haven't skipped the last one.
    - You cannot heal twice in a room. Only the first instance will be counted.

Donsol rules:
    - Standard deck with 2 jokers.
    - Starting and max HP is 21.
    - J is 11, Q 13, K 15, A 17 and Jokers are 21.
    - A room is 4 cards.
    - Diamonds are shields (unlike swords, shields are destructible) and Hearts are potions (can be used multiple times).
    - Diamond and Heart cards have J,Q,K,A equal to 11 instead.
    - (Easy) Room is escapable if the monsters have been dealt with or player hasn't escaped the last room.
    - (Normal) Room is escapable if the player hasnt escaped the last room.
    - (Hard) Room is escapable only when all monsters have been dealt with.
    - Escaped room will put all the cards at the back of the deck.
